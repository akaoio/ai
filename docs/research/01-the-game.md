# 01 — The Game

Texas Hold'em is a community-card poker variant played between 2 to 9 players at a single table. Each player competes to win chips by either holding the best five-card hand at showdown, or by making all other players fold before a showdown is reached.

Each player is dealt two private cards — called **hole cards** — that only they can see. Five **community cards** are then dealt face-up in the center of the table and are shared by all players. A player may use any combination of their two hole cards and the five community cards to construct the best possible five-card hand. It is entirely valid to use both hole cards, only one, or even none (playing the board), though playing the board means every other remaining player also has access to the same five-card hand.

---

## The Flow of a Hand

A complete hand of Texas Hold'em progresses through the following stages in order:

**1. Dealing hole cards (Preflop)**
Two cards are dealt face-down to each player, starting from the player immediately left of the dealer button and going clockwise. Once all players have two cards, the first betting round begins. The player to the left of the big blind acts first preflop. Action proceeds clockwise until all remaining players have contributed equal amounts to the pot or folded.

**2. The Flop**
Three community cards are dealt face-up simultaneously in the center of the table. These are available to all players. A second betting round begins, this time starting with the first active player to the left of the dealer button. This positional convention — acting from left of the button — applies for all remaining streets.

**3. The Turn**
A single fourth community card is dealt face-up. A third betting round follows under the same rules.

**4. The River**
A single fifth and final community card is dealt face-up. The fourth and final betting round takes place. If more than one player remains after this round of betting, a showdown occurs.

**5. Showdown**
All remaining players reveal their hole cards. The player who can form the highest-ranking five-card hand using any combination of their hole cards and the five community cards wins the pot. In the event of a tie, the pot is split equally between the tied players.

If at any point during the hand all players except one fold, the last remaining player wins the entire pot immediately without needing to show their cards.

---

## The Dealer Button

The dealer button (often just called "the button") is a disc that rotates clockwise around the table after each hand. It marks who acts as the nominal dealer. In live poker, a professional dealer handles the cards and the button simply determines position. In online poker, the button player is the last to act on every postflop street. Being on the button is the strongest position at the table because the button player has full information of all other players' actions before making a decision.

---

## Number of Players

Texas Hold'em can be played:

- **Full ring**: 8–9 players
- **6-max**: 6 players — a very common format, especially online
- **Heads-up**: 2 players — a highly specific and specialized game where dynamics change significantly (the button is also the small blind and acts first preflop but last postflop)

The number of active players affects everything: hand strength requirements, bluffing frequencies, range construction, and stack pressure.
